Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Crayon Kid, by Mehyar Soft

🖼️ Your photos

When you use "Photo → coloring", your uploaded photo is processed in memory only to draw your coloring page — it is never stored, never logged, and never shared. The original photo is discarded the moment your page is drawn.

Two things we do keep, briefly, and only so your book works:

  • The coloring page we drew is cached for a short time (up to 7 days for photo redraws, up to 30 days for theme/described pages) so we can show it on the page and embed it in your free-page email. It expires automatically.
  • A one-sentence text description of the photo (e.g. "a golden retriever puppy sitting in grass") is kept up to 30 days so your paid 12-page book can be themed on it.

📧 Your email and your kid's name

We ask for the parent's email and the kid's first name when you make a free page. We use them to:

  • Email you the free page and a link to come back and finish checkout later.
  • Remember your book setup (name, theme, detail level) so you don't retype it.
  • Send your purchase receipt and download link after you pay.

No spam, ever. We don't send newsletters or marketing emails — only the emails above, tied to something you started.

💳 Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see, store, or touch your card number — Stripe handles all of it under Stripe's privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that your $6 purchase succeeded, plus the email you gave us.

📊 Other data

We count how many pages are generated per IP address (a one-hour rolling counter) to stop abuse — that's it. No accounts, no cookies for tracking, no analytics that identify you.

🚫 What we never do

We never sell your data. We never share your photos (they're never stored at all), your email, or your kid's name with anyone except what's needed to run the service (Stripe for payments, our email provider for your receipts).

🗑️ Your rights

Want your email deleted, or have any question about your data? Write to info@mehyar.us and we'll take care of it.
