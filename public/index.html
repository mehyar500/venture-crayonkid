Questions parents ask

Can I turn my own photo into a coloring page?

Yes! Choose "Photo → coloring", upload a picture of their pet, toy, or favorite outing, and we'll redraw it as a coloring page — free. Your photo is only used to draw the page and is never stored.

How good is the print quality?

The $6 PDF is high-resolution print quality — crisp on US Letter or A4 paper, regular paper or cardstock. (Straight talk: AI art is high-res raster, not vector, so it's made for printing, not billboards.)

How do I print it?

The PDF is US Letter size — just hit print from any computer or phone. Regular paper works great; cardstock makes it extra nice.

Is the $6 really one-time?

Yes. Pay once, download the PDF, print it as many times as you like, forever. No subscription, no upsells.

What ages is it for?

Best for ages 3–8. Use the detail level picker: Simple gives big bold outlines for ages 3–5, Detailed gives intricate patterns for ages 6+.

Can I make one for each of my kids?

Absolutely — each book is personalized with one name. Siblings each get their own starring role. 🌟
